# B11 board run: ina2xx on a CM5

Status: untested on board. The register layer and the driver are covered by unit tests on the
simulated bus (`backends/sim`). No transfer has reached a real INA226, INA238 or INA260.

Setup: one monitor wired to a CM5 I2C bus, with a board file device such as:

```toml
[[devices]]
id = "power0"
driver = "ina2xx"
bus = "i2c-N"
config = { chip = "ina238", address = 0x40, shunt_ohms = 0.010, max_current_a = 2.0 }
```

The INA260 ignores the shunt and current keys. Use the matching entry for the chip fitted.

1. Build the driver for aarch64 and bind the device with the chip present.
   Pass: bind succeeds. Health is `Degraded` until the first read, then `Ok`.
2. Read the device with no load on the rail.
   Pass: `bus_v` matches the supply within 0.5 V, and `current_a` is within 2 mA of zero.
3. Apply a known load, and compare `current_a` and `power_w` with a bench meter.
   Pass: `current_a` is within 2% of the meter reading, and `power_w` is within 2% of volts × amps.
4. Read the calibration register over the bus with `i2cget` (0x02 for INA238, 0x05 for INA226).
   Pass: the INA238 value is 2000 (0x07d0) at 10 mΩ and 2 A. The INA226 value is 2048 (0x0800) at
   10 mΩ and 8.192 A.
5. Disconnect the chip's I2C wiring during reads, then reconnect it.
   Pass: reads fail with an error and health shows `Degraded` while disconnected. After
   reconnecting, readings resume without a restart, and the calibration register is written again.
6. Point the device at an address nothing answers (for example 0x41).
   Pass: bind succeeds and reads fail. The driver keeps accepting reads and does not switch itself
   off. Retry pacing is the host's, and it stays within the 8 s cap.
7. Configure `chip = "ina226"` against an INA238.
   Pass: reads fail as a wrong chip (`NOT_SUPPORTED`), and no calibration write reaches the chip.
8. Time 1000 reads of an idle chip on the CM5, and record the median and worst case here.
   Pass: every read is a single I2C transaction (one `transaction` call per poll), and the timings
   are recorded. CM5 timings only.

## Run on the CM5, 2026-11-17 (Raze #2, boot 0f80a3d6-6aa1-4814-ba3d-800bfff5dd06)

The monitor is an INA238 at 0x40 on `i2c-1` (the bench board file's `power0`). Our `lemnosd` ran alone under the failsafe;
the image's services were restored and compared with the baseline.

- Check 1: pass. Bind with the chip present: `running ok`, `power-monitor/1,self-test/1`, at the first list and after 30 s.
- Check 2 (bus voltage with no load): the chip's own register reads 4.5 V. `i2cget -y 1 0x40 0x05 w` gives `0xa205` (byte-
  swapped), i.e. 0x05a2 = 1442 counts, and 1442 x 3.125 mV = 4.5 V. But `lemnos-ctl read power0` prints
  `bus_voltage = 0.0000000045 V`, which is 1e9 too small. The driver's item is declared in volts with exponent -9
  (`drivers/ina2xx/src/lib.rs`, `describe`), and the value that reaches the formatter is in volts. So the display path
  (or the item's value) is wrong by 1e9. The `bus_v` field is computed as `bus_voltage_uv as f32 * 1e-6`, which is 4.5 V.
  Recorded as a bug, not fixed here. The check is not passed on the `read` output.
- Check 3 (load): not run, needs a meter and a load.
- Check 4 (calibration register): pass. `i2cget -y 1 0x40 0x02 w` gives `0xd007`, i.e. 0x07d0 = 2000, the INA238 value at
  10 mOhm and 2 A.
- Check 5 (unplug): not run, a hands step.
- Check 6 (no device at 0x41): not run in this session.
- Check 7 (`chip = "ina226"` against an INA238): not run.
- Check 8 (timings): not run.

Restore: lemnosd and lemnos-orion active, `systemctl --failed` 0, helios-engine active, the failsafe stopped.

### Hands list (from the 2026-11-17 run)

- **Current reads -0.369 A with no load.** `lemnos-ctl read power0` (after the scale fix on pr/INA-UNITS, 13c82a0f)
  gives `current = -0.369 A` and `shunt_voltage = -0.0037 V`. The raw registers agree (shunt register and current
  register both show it), so the driver is reading what the chip measures; the chip is measuring a current in the
  reverse direction, or the shunt is wired other than the board file says. Check 2 of this record wants the current
  within 2 mA of zero: NOT MET. Needs a bench meter on the rail and a look at the shunt wiring (hands).
- Check 3 (load against a meter), check 5 (unplug the I2C wiring and reconnect), check 6 (an address nothing answers,
  such as 0x41, with the device configured there), check 7 (`chip = "ina226"` against an INA238) and check 8 (timings
  with the chip idle): not run; checks 3 and 5 need hands, 6 and 7 need a board file with a second device or a chip
  swap, 8 needs only time, which this session did not spend.
- Known gap, not a hands item: the bus voltage's fractional warning limit (`bus_warn_below_v = 10.8`) is not in the
  item descriptor; an item's limit is a whole number of its unit. Pending the ruling on the unit change (see the INA
  fix's commit message).
