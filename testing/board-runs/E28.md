# E28 board run: the static host and the MCU backend on a microcontroller

Status: untested on board. `engine/host-static` and `backends/mcu` are tested on the host
against doubles (an embedded-hal register-file bus for the BMI088, a USB device port and a USB host
port), and the crates build for `thumbv7em-none-eabihf` and `riscv32imac-unknown-none-elf`. No
firmware built on them has run on a microcontroller, and no USB transfer has crossed a real wire.

Setup: a Cortex-M4F (or RISC-V) board with a USB device port and a BMI088 on I2C at its default
addresses (0x18 and 0x68), and a PC on its USB port. The firmware supplies a `Lock` (a critical
section around a `RefCell`), a `HostClock` from a hardware timer, `lemnos_mcu::I2cBus` over its
HAL's I2C, and a `lemnos_mcu::usb::FunctionPort` over its USB peripheral. Build on the NVMe or SSD,
never on the HDD.

Table: `sensor` (`bmi088`, bus `i2c0`) and `fused` (`orientation`, uses `accel` and `gyro` =
`sensor`, `sample_rate_hz = 100`), the parent declared first.

1. Flash and boot. Pass: both devices reach `Running` (sensor first), and the first orientation
   read answers within 20 reads.
2. Read `fused` at 100 Hz for 60 s with the board flat and still. Pass: every read after power-up
   answers, roll and pitch within 0.5 degrees of zero.
3. Pull the BMI088's SDA for 2 s during reads, then reconnect. Pass: reads fail during the outage,
   `health(fused)` is not `Ok`, and reads answer again after reconnecting with no reset of the
   firmware.
4. Boot with the BMI088 unplugged, plug it in after 20 s. Pass: `sensor` binds at once (its bind
   does not touch the bus) and reads fail until the chip answers, then succeed; nothing waits more
   than 8 s between retries.
5. Enable a `lemnos_mcu::usb::Function` with a vendor-class configuration (one bulk IN, one bulk
   OUT endpoint, two strings). Pass: the PC enumerates it (`lsusb -v` shows the descriptors and
   strings exactly as given), and 1000 bulk packets of 64 bytes echo back unchanged.
6. Record the linked image's flash and RAM use (`size` on the ELF) and the stack high-water mark
   during `TABLE.start` (paint the stack before boot). Compare with the figures in the A32 PR
   (39 744 bytes of flash and 6 932 bytes of static RAM on `thumbv7em-none-eabihf`, without the
   USB function; the bmi088 bind's stack frame is 6 016 bytes).
7. Rebinding (A32). The buses are in `lemnos_mcu::Stall`s. With `fused` being read at 100 Hz, pull
   the BMI088's SDA for 30 s. Pass: after five failed reads `state(sensor)` is `Backoff`, the
   firmware keeps running, and nothing else changes. Reconnect. Pass: `sensor` is `Running` again
   within 8 s of a retry coming due, `fused` answers again with no reset, and repeating the pull
   ten times leaves the stack high-water mark and static RAM where they were.
