# B4: Hotplug

Status: untested on board. The inotify and rescan sources are tested on a fake sysfs tree, and the
uevent parser on recorded kernel messages. Netlink has not seen a real hotplug.

On the CM5, with a USB device to plug and unplug:

1. Open a `DeviceWatcher` for `usb` and coldplug it.
   Pass: every present device arrives as `add`.
2. Plug the device in, then pull it out.
   Pass: `add` and `bind`, then `unbind` and `remove`, with the expected devpath.
3. Flood the socket by rebinding a device in a loop with the reader stopped, then read.
   Pass: an `Overflow` arrives, and a coldplug gives the correct list.
