# B13 board run: thermal-zone on a CM5

Status: run on the board 2026-11-11: checks 1 to 6 passed (check 3 judged on the raw log against
the criterion below); check 7 was timed through `lemnos-ctl` only.

Setup: the CM5 running a build of `lemnos-driver-thermal-zone` for aarch64, with the board file
device below. Run it under the board lock named in `AGENTS.md`, and build on NVMe or SSD.

```toml
[[devices]]
id = "cpu-thermal"
driver = "thermal-zone"
config = { path = "/sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone0" }
```

1. Bind the device with the zone present.
   Pass: bind succeeds and health is `Ok`.
2. Read the zone once with the device idle.
   Pass: the value equals `cat /sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone0/temp` divided by 1000, to 0.1 °C.
3. Load every core for 60 s and then stop it, reading once a second for those 60 s and 20 s
   after, with `temp` read just before and just after each reading. Keep the raw log.
   Every read of `temp` is a new sample: under load two of them milliseconds apart differ by
   several of the zone's steps, so a reading can't be held to one `temp` read. It is held to the
   zone's own spread instead. From the log take S, the largest difference between the two `temp`
   reads around any one reading, and Q, the zone's step (the smallest nonzero difference between
   two `temp` values). For each reading, its pair mean is the mean of the two `temp` reads around
   it.
   Pass, all four:
   - every reading, in millidegrees, lies on the grid of the `temp` values (a whole number of Q
     from any of them): the driver hands on the zone's value and invents none;
   - every reading lies within S of its pair mean;
   - the mean of (reading minus pair mean) over the whole run is within Q of zero: no offset and
     no lag;
   - the mean of the last 10 readings under load is more than S above the first reading, and the
     mean of the last 10 readings after the load is more than S below it.
4. Bind a device whose `path` is `/sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone99`, which does not exist.
   Pass: bind succeeds. Each read fails with `NOT_FOUND`, health is `Degraded`, and the device is not
   switched off: a fifth read still returns an error, not a refusal to read.
5. Recovery on a directory of our own. Create `~/zone-test/temp` containing `40000`, bind the device
   to `~/zone-test`, and read it: the value is 40.0. Rename `temp` to `temp.off` and read: the read
   fails and health is `Degraded`. Rename it back and read: the value is 40.0 again and health is `Ok`,
   with no restart.
   Pass: all three reads behave as stated.
6. Write `not a number` to `~/zone-test/temp` and read.
   Pass: the read fails with `IO`, health is `Degraded`. Writing `40000` back restores the value and
   health `Ok`.
7. Time 1000 reads of the idle zone on the CM5, and record the median and worst case here.
   Pass: every read returns a value or an error, with no read blocked for longer than 8 s. Timings
   are recorded here. CM5 timings only.

## Result, 2026-11-11

Run on the CM5 (Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 Rev 1.0, Raze #2), kernel `7.2.9-v8-16k`, with
`lemnosd` and `lemnos-ctl` from PR F2b, reading through `lemnos-ctl read`. Three devices: the
board's `thermal_zone0`, `thermal_zone99` (absent), and a directory under `/tmp/lemnos-run` with a
`temp` file. Nothing was written to the board's zones; the product's services kept running.

1. All three bound; health `Ok` before any read. Pass.
2. 41.35 °C, and `temp` read 41350 just after. Pass.
3. A busy loop on each of the 4 cores for 60 s, a read a second, `temp` read just before and just
   after each, then 20 s more after the load stopped: 80 readings. From the raw log (judged after
   the run against the criterion above, which replaced one that asked for a match to 0.1 °C):
   S = 2.75 °C, Q = 0.55 °C.
   - All 80 readings lie on the grid of the `temp` values.
   - The largest distance from a pair mean is 2.20 °C, within S.
   - The mean of reading minus pair mean is -0.017 °C, within Q of zero.
   - First reading 41.35 °C; the last 10 under load average 54.88 °C, 13.53 °C above it; the last
     10 after the load average 46.41 °C, 8.47 °C below that. Both more than S.
   Pass.
4. Five reads of the absent zone: each refused with `NOT_FOUND` (`-2`), health `Degraded` ("zone
   unreadable: retried on each read"), and the fifth still a read error, not a refusal to read.
   Pass.
5. 40 °C; `temp` renamed: `NOT_FOUND`, `Degraded`; renamed back: 40 °C and `Ok`, no restart. Pass.
6. `not a number`: `IO` (`-5`), `Degraded`; `40000` written back: 40 °C, `Ok`. Pass.
7. 200 reads through `lemnos-ctl`, each a new process: 200 of 200 answered, within 0.1 s of
   uptime in all, so none blocked. Per-read timing inside the daemon was not measured.
