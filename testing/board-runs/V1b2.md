# V1b2: the ov9782 exposure, gain and frame length items, on the reference board

Untested on board. What the driver does with a set through an item is shown at the driver level
on the mock I2C bus and a scripted receiver (`drivers/ov9782/src/tests.rs`). Nothing here has run
on a sensor.

## The check

On the reference board, with the OV9782 streaming mode 0 through its receiver:

1. `lemnos-ctl show <sensor>`: the items `exposure` (value and control), `frame_length` (value and
   control) and `analogue_gain` (value and control) appear. The exposure and frame length controls
   have a range and step 1 with unit `line`; the analogue gain control has the range 16 to 255
   and step 1; before a mode is selected the exposure and frame length ranges are absent.
2. Read `exposure` and `frame_length` (value items): they equal the mode's defaults (642 lines and
   the mode's default frame length, from `in_force`).
3. Set `exposure` to 100 lines through its control. The answer is a landing frame `F`. Count the
   receiver's frames from the start; the exposure must change on frame `F` and not before. Repeat
   with `analogue_gain` 32 and `frame_length` 1000; the frame length must not shorten the frame
   before exposure is written (`set_controls` ordering, unchanged).
4. Stop the sensor and set `exposure` again: the answer must be `INVALID_ARGUMENT` and the bus must
   see no write.

## What to record

The landing frame for each set, the frame the image changes on, and whether they agree. A
disagreement is the J4b and D10 question again, now through the item path; record it in this file.
