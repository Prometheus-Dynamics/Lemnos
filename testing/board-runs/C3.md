# C3: I2C and SPI through the module, on a board

Untested on board. The QEMU run covers the transfer paths against `i2c-stub`, a memory-backed test adapter and a loopback controller.

On the CM5, A/B against `i2c-dev` and `spidev`, same bus, same target, same transfer shape:

1. I2C: a write-then-read of a register on a real device, many times, through `/dev/i2c-N` and through `LEMNOS_IOC_I2C_TRANSFER`. Same data both ways; record time per transfer.
2. SPI: full-duplex transfers on a real device or a jumpered MOSI to MISO, through `spidev` and through `LEMNOS_IOC_SPI_TRANSFER` with the device bound through `driver_override`. Same data; record time per transfer.
3. An address held by a kernel driver gets `EBUSY`.
