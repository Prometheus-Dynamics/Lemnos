## Build

- `no_std`. Re-exports embedded-hal for I2C, SPI, GPIO, PWM and delays.
- Keeps `RegisterBus`, `Regulator`, `ClockOutput`, `RawLine`, `RawPwm`, `RawSpi`, `HalError`.
- Adds `Registers`, `Interrupt`, `DmaBuffers`, `Clocks`, `Reset`, `StreamEndpoint`.
- Adds USB, both roles: `UsbHost`/`UsbDevice` (talking to something plugged in) and `UsbFunction` (this board appearing as a USB device).

## Proof

A mock for each trait with its own tests. Builds for the three bare-metal targets in A3.
