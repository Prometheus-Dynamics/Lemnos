## Build

- `engine/*` may not depend on `contracts/*`, `drivers/*`, `backends/*` or `service/*`.
- No type or module in `engine/` or `service/` may be named after hardware: camera, imu, sensor, gyro, accel, fan, light, led, isp.
- `unsafe` only in `engine/abi`, `engine/capi`, `backends/linux-sys` and crates named `drivers/*-sys`.
- No product names, except in the planning files (`AGENTS.md`, `TODO.md`, `docs/design.md`, `docs/prs/`), which F1 removes or rewrites.

## Proof

Each rule has a planted-violation fixture that makes the check fail.
