## Build

- Programs the ISP8000 and CSI directly through `/dev/vvcam-isp.0`. The vendor `isp_media_server` daemon is never used.
- Formats: NV12, NV16, YUYV, BGR24, P010, 8, 10 and 12-bit Bayer.
- The SDK never sets `v4l2_buffer.sequence` or the timestamp, so the driver supplies its own.
